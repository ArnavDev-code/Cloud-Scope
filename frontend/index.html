03 / METHODOLOGY Behind the estimate

Cloud costs aren't evenly distributed.

A small number of expensive workloads can pull the average far above what a typical workload costs. Here, the historical mean is about ₹117.9K, while the median is about ₹46.2K.

Instead of relying on one average, CloudScope predicts the 5th, 50th, and 95th cost percentiles, then calibrates the interval using Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR). The result is a useful planning range that makes uncertainty visible.

TRAINING DATA 124,275 Historical billing records
INTERVAL COVERAGE 90.4% Held-out calibration results
MEDIAN MODEL FIT 0.665 R² in log space
PREDICTION METHOD P05 / P50 / P95 Quantile XGBoost + CQR

Limitations worth knowing

The training data covers 2022–2023 and may not reflect current cloud-provider pricing, product changes, discounts, taxes, or regional price differences. Historical interval coverage is a validation result, not a guarantee for every future workload. Always verify actual charges with your cloud provider before making spending commitments.
